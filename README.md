One sentence, up to 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life — for the [Everyday Wonder](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3) bounty on make.fun.

## The entry

> At the warung, Ibu Sari poured my coffee into a glass that still held yesterday's rain, and the two weathers mixed without asking.

130 characters, one sentence. A named person, a specific place, one sensory detail, and one small turn at the end — the everyday made strange in a single read.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
